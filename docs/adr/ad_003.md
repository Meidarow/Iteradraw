# Thumbnailing

## Context and Problem Statement

In Iteradraw, thumbnailing is the central optimization _and_ standardization strategie,
allowing the app to keep its profile small, even when large images are being used
due to only having a single full-resolution image loaded at any given time, 
and load time under control.

The thumbnailing occurs immediately after an image is decoded, and only the 2K version
of the image ordinarily is rendered on screen (exception: ZOOM).
Every Thumbnail is held in a central database for easy access; they are saved as
lossy WEBPs to harness its fast decodes and small file-size compromise.
Each thumbnail is approximately 70KB only, allowing the app to use disk-storage 
to compensate for its frugality.

The thumbnail cache may be cleared by the user at any time, this action simply
resets the database; manual deletion of said database achieves the same end.
